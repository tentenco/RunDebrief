# RunDebrief Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the RunDebrief community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, religion, sexual identity or orientation, or technology preference.

We will act and interact in ways that support an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- showing empathy and respect for different experiences and viewpoints;
- giving and accepting constructive, specific feedback;
- taking responsibility, apologizing when appropriate, and learning from mistakes;
- protecting private data and using synthetic examples in public reports;
- focusing on what is best for the community and the people affected by the software.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- posting transcripts, credentials, customer identifiers, or private machine paths;
- conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when an individual officially represents RunDebrief or Tenten in public spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

For conduct reports, use [Tenten's contact form](https://tenten.co/contact) and include `RunDebrief community conduct` in the subject or first line. Do not include credentials, private transcripts, or unnecessary personal data. If the form itself is unsuitable for sensitive details, request a private follow-up channel first.

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and may apply a temporary or permanent ban when warranted.

Maintainers will respect the privacy and security of reporters as far as reasonably possible. False reports made in bad faith may themselves violate this Code of Conduct.
